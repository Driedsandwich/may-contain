# Staff-card translation review

The eight staff cards use fixed templates and explicit allergen names in `web/content.ts`. No text is generated by AI at runtime. These prototype phrases have not been independently reviewed by native speakers or clinical interpreters. Chinese uses simplified characters. Use the English meaning to verify your selection.

Before a public production release, ask a native speaker with food-allergy terminology experience to review every template and all 20 names in their language. Review singular and plural selections, broad groups versus specific foods, and one/all-allergen cards. Record reviewer role, language, date, changes and approval in this file without personal identifiers. Do not treat an unreviewed language as clinically validated.

To correct a phrase, edit `CARD_COPY`, the corresponding names and any French preposition in `web/content.ts`; run `npm test`, `npm run build` and `npm run test:ui`. Confirm that English meaning, selected IDs and displayed local names agree. Recheck the card on a 390px screen, in landscape and with enlarged text. The card asks staff a question; it must never give permission to eat.
